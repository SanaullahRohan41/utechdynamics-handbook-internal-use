## Room Booking Workflow

### 1. **User Registration & Login**
   - Create an account with email and password
   - Login to access the booking dashboard
   - Manage your profile and booking history

### 2. **Search Available Rooms**
   - Select check-in and check-out dates
   - Choose number of guests
   - Specify room preferences (single, double, suite, etc.)
   - View filtered results based on availability and price

### 3. **Browse Room Details**
   - Review room descriptions and amenities
   - View high-quality room images
   - Check pricing per night
   - See cancellation policies and terms

### 4. **Select Room & Review Booking**
   - Click "Book Now" on desired room
   - Review booking summary:
     - Room type and configuration
     - Check-in/Check-out dates
     - Number of nights
     - Total price breakdown
   - Add special requests (late arrival, extra bed, etc.)

### 5. **Enter Guest Information**
   - Primary guest details (name, phone, email)
   - Additional guest information if applicable
   - Billing address
   - Emergency contact details

### 6. **Payment Processing**
   - Select payment method (credit card, debit card, online wallet)
   - Enter payment details securely
   - Review total amount including taxes and fees
   - Process payment

### 7. **Booking Confirmation**
   - Receive booking confirmation on screen
   - Confirmation email sent to registered email
   - Booking reference number provided
   - Download or print booking details

### 8. **Post-Booking Management**
   - View active bookings in dashboard
   - Modify booking if allowed
   - Cancel booking if needed
   - Add special requests
   - Contact hotel support if necessary

### 9. **Check-In**
   - Arrive at hotel with booking reference
   - Present valid ID at reception
   - Complete check-in formalities
   - Receive room key/card
   - Review room inventory and report issues immediately

### 10. **Stay & Check-Out**
   - Enjoy hotel amenities and services
   - Request housekeeping or front desk assistance
   - Complete check-out before deadline
   - Settle additional charges (if any)
   - Provide feedback/reviews
