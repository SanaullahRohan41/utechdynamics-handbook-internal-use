# Common Abbreviations in Software Business

## Office & Workplace Abbreviations

- **[DRY](ca://s?q=Explain_DRY)** — Don’t Repeat Yourself (principle to avoid duplication)  
- **[KISS](ca://s?q=Explain_KISS)** — Keep It Simple, Stupid (design principle for simplicity)  
- **[FYI](ca://s?q=Explain_FYI)** — For Your Information  
- **[FYR](ca://s?q=Explain_FYR)** — For Your Reference  
- **[ETA](ca://s?q=Explain_ETA)** — Estimated Time of Arrival  
- **[ASAP](ca://s?q=Explain_ASAP)** — As Soon As Possible  
- **[OOO](ca://s?q=Explain_OOO)** — Out Of Office  
- **[EOD](ca://s?q=Explain_EOD)** — End Of Day  
- **[COB](ca://s?q=Explain_COB)** — Close Of Business  
- **[PFA](ca://s?q=Explain_PFA)** — Please Find Attached  
- **[TBD](ca://s?q=Explain_TBD)** — To Be Determined  
- **[RSVP](ca://s?q=Explain_RSVP)** — Répondez S’il Vous Plaît (Please Respond)
